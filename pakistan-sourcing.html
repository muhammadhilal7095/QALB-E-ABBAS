Why source your products from Pakistan?

Pakistan is a major exporter of cotton textiles, towels and bedding, leather goods, sports equipment, surgical and beauty instruments, and Himalayan pink salt. Many factories in Sialkot, Faisalabad, Karachi and Lahore already make products for international brands and can produce private label orders at competitive prices.

The risk is in the details: traders posing as factories, samples that don't match the final order, weak packaging, missing certificates and surprise shipping costs. We check the supplier, approve samples with you, inspect the goods before they leave Pakistan and arrange delivery, so you know what you're paying for.

Product sourcing from Pakistan: FAQs

What products are best to source from Pakistan?

Pakistan is strongest in cotton textiles, towels and bedding, leather goods and garments, sports equipment such as footballs and gloves, surgical and beauty instruments, and Himalayan pink salt products. We tell you honestly if your product is better made elsewhere.

Where are the main manufacturing cities?

Sialkot is known for sports goods, leather and surgical instruments; Faisalabad for cotton textiles and home textiles; Karachi and Lahore for garments, leather and a wide range of other products. We search the cities that make your type of product.

How do you check that a Pakistani supplier is reliable?

We check the company's registration and export history, visit or video-call the factory, review its production capacity and ask for references, then order samples before you commit to a full order.

Can I order samples first?

Yes, and we recommend it. Samples are sent to you so you can check materials, size, stitching, finish and packaging. Production only starts once you've approved a sample.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on the product. Many Pakistani manufacturers accept smaller first orders for textiles, leather and sports goods than large factories elsewhere. We negotiate MOQ and price together so the first order fits your budget.

Do you inspect goods before shipping?

Yes. Before the balance is paid, random units are checked for defects, quantity, labels and packing, and you receive photos and a written report. Problems are fixed before the goods leave Pakistan.

Can you make private label products with my brand?

Yes. We arrange your logo on the product, labels, swing tags and boxes, plus barcodes and Amazon FNSKU labels if you sell on Amazon.

How are goods shipped from Pakistan?

Most orders ship by sea from Karachi, or by air or express courier for smaller and urgent orders, to the UK, US, UAE, Europe or an Amazon warehouse. We arrange the freight forwarder and paperwork.

Who pays import duty and tax?

Import duty and VAT or sales tax are paid in your country, usually by you as the importer. Some Pakistani goods qualify for lower duty under trade schemes in the UK and EU; we explain the likely costs before you order.

How much does your Pakistan sourcing service cost?

Start with a $50 consultation to plan your order. Our sourcing fee is then quoted in writing based on the product, quantity and services you need. Product, shipping and import costs are separate and explained up front.
