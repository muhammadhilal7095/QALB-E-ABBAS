Why sell on Amazon?

Amazon is the world's largest online marketplace, with hundreds of millions of active customers and Prime members who buy fast and often. With Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA), Amazon stores, packs and ships your products and handles customer service, so you can focus on products and growth.

FAQs

Amazon Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Amazon FBA and FBM?

With FBA, Amazon stores, packs and ships your orders and handles customer service, and your listings get Prime. With FBM you store and ship orders yourself. Most new private label sellers start with FBA.

Can I sell on Amazon if I live outside the US or UK?

Yes. Many sellers from Pakistan, the UAE and elsewhere sell on Amazon US and UK. You'll need valid ID, a matching address and a bank or payout account; a US LLC or UK LTD often makes approval and banking easier.

How much money do I need to start on Amazon?

It depends on your product, but plan for samples, your first inventory order, shipping, Amazon fees, PPC and a reserve for reorders. Our free Amazon assessment helps you check whether your budget is safe.

How long does Amazon account verification take?

Usually a few days to a few weeks. Matching, current documents are the biggest factor — we prepare and check them with you before you apply.

What does the Q1–Q4 product research include?

Each quarter we research 20 high-demand products for that season, with demand, competition, estimated margins and supplier options, so you can choose a product with evidence.

What is Amazon Brand Registry and why does it matter?

Brand Registry gives a trademark owner more control over their listings, access to A+ Content, Sponsored Brands ads and a Storefront, and tools to report copycats and hijackers. You need a registered or pending trademark to enrol.

Do foreign sellers need a US attorney for Amazon Brand Registry?

A US trademark application from a foreign applicant must be filed by a US-licensed attorney. Once your trademark is filed you can apply for Amazon Brand Registry.

How long until my Amazon product becomes profitable?

It varies with the product, price and competition. Most launches need a few months of PPC, reviews and reorders before ranking settles. We plan your launch budget so you know what to expect before you order stock.

My Amazon account is suspended — can you help?

Yes. We review the suspension notice and your account health, then write a clear plan of action with the right supporting documents to appeal.
