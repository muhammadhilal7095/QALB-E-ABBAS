FAQs

Amazon FBA FAQs

What is the difference between Amazon FBA and FBM?

FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) means Amazon stores, packs, ships, and provides customer service for your products, making your listings eligible for Prime shipping. FBM (Fulfillment by Merchant) requires you or your 3PL to ship orders directly to customers.

Do foreign sellers need a US attorney for Amazon Brand Registry?

Yes. To enroll in Brand Registry, you must have a pending or registered trademark. Non-US residents filing a USPTO trademark are legally required under 37 CFR § 2.11 to hire a US-licensed state bar attorney. See our trademark registration services.
