Overview

Why Study in Malta?

Malta offers international students opportunities to pursue education in a European environment, develop practical skills, and experience a diverse international community.

  • Explore educational opportunities in Europe.
  • Choose from a range of academic and professional courses.
  • Study in an English-speaking environment at suitable institutions.
  • Gain international educational experience.
  • Develop skills for future academic and career opportunities.
  • Experience Malta's culture and student lifestyle.

Eligibility depends on the institution, course requirements, academic qualifications, and applicable immigration rules.
