FAQs

App Development FAQs

How long does it take to build an app?

A simple app can take a few weeks, while feature-rich apps take several months. We give you a timeline after the discovery and planning stage.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on features, design and platforms. After discussing your idea we send a clear quote with a breakdown of what's included.

Should I build for Android, iOS or both?

Most businesses need both. Cross-platform development builds one app for Android and iOS, saving time and cost.

Will you publish my app on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes. We prepare the store listings and handle submission. The developer accounts are set up in your name.

Who owns the app?

Your app, store accounts and content are set up in your name, and ownership and handover are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Can you add payments, logins and notifications?

Yes. We can add secure payments, user accounts, push notifications, booking, chat and other features your business needs.

Do you design the app as well?

Yes. UI/UX design is part of our process, so you see and approve the screens before development starts.

Can you improve or redesign my existing app?

Yes. We redesign, fix and add features to existing apps.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance for updates, bug fixes, new features and compatibility with new Android and iOS versions.
