Introduction

What is email marketing?

Email marketing lets you talk directly to customers and subscribers in their inbox — a channel you own, not one controlled by a social media algorithm. It's one of the most cost-effective ways to drive repeat sales and nurture new leads.

The biggest wins come from automation: welcome sequences, abandoned-cart reminders and post-purchase flows that send the right message at the right time, every time. We plan, design and build these flows, plus regular campaigns and newsletters, and track every open, click and sale.

FAQs

Email Marketing FAQs

Which email platforms do you work with?

We work with popular platforms such as Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Brevo and Shopify Email, and recommend the best fit for your business.

Do you buy or rent email lists?

No. We only email people who have agreed to hear from you. This protects your sender reputation and keeps you compliant with privacy laws such as GDPR.

How often should I send emails?

Most businesses do well with one to four emails a month, plus automated emails triggered by customer actions. We plan a schedule that suits your audience.

Which automated emails do you set up?

Common flows include welcome emails, abandoned cart reminders, post-purchase follow-ups, review requests and win-back emails.

How do you stop emails going to spam?

We set up domain authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), clean your list and write emails that inbox providers trust.

Can you help me grow my email list?

Yes. We create sign-up forms, pop-ups and lead magnets that turn website visitors into subscribers.

Do you design the email templates?

Yes. We design branded, mobile-friendly templates that match your website.

How do you measure results?

We track opens, clicks, conversions and revenue, and report what is working and what we will improve next.

Is email marketing still effective?

Yes. Email is a channel you own, and it is one of the most cost-effective ways to bring customers back and increase repeat sales.
