Introduction

What is social media marketing?

Social media marketing (SMM) promotes your business on platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn. It builds brand awareness, keeps you in front of customers and turns followers into enquiries and sales.

Success comes from consistency and the right content for each platform. We plan a monthly content calendar, create posts, Reels and short videos, manage comments and messages, and report on what's working — so your social media becomes a real growth channel.

Social Media Marketing FAQs

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn. We focus on the platforms where your customers spend time.

Do you create the content?

Yes. We design posts, write captions and plan reels and short videos that fit your brand.

How often will you post?

It depends on your plan and platforms. We agree a content calendar with you in advance.

Do I approve content before it's posted?

Yes. You review the content calendar and posts before they go live.

Is paid advertising included?

Social media management covers organic content and engagement. Paid ads are available through our Digital Marketing service, and ad spend is paid separately.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

Yes. Community management, including replying to comments and messages, can be part of your plan.

How long does it take to see growth?

Most accounts see better engagement within the first few months of consistent, quality posting.

Do you guarantee followers?

No. We never buy followers or use fake engagement. We grow real audiences that can become customers.

How do you report results?

You receive regular reports on reach, engagement, followers and website clicks, with recommendations.
