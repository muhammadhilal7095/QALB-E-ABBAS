Introduction

What is TikTok automation?

TikTok automation uses TikTok's built-in business tools and approved integrations to handle repetitive work on your account. Welcome messages, keyword replies and suggested questions answer DMs instantly, while comment filters keep your videos clean and spam-free.

Automation also connects TikTok to the rest of your business: lead generation forms send customer details to your email, Google Sheets or CRM, and scheduled posting keeps your content consistent. We never use follow, like or view bots that break TikTok's rules and put accounts at risk.
