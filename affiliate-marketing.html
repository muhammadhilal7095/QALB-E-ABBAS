Introduction

What is affiliate marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based channel where partners — bloggers, review sites, creators and publishers — promote your products and earn a commission on each sale or lead they bring in. You only pay when results happen.

A successful program needs fair commissions, reliable tracking, clear rules and active partners. We set up your program on the right platform, recruit relevant affiliates and manage the program — or build affiliate websites and content if you want to earn as an affiliate yourself.

  • ModelCommission on sales or leads
  • TrackingAffiliate software & networks
  • PartnersBloggers, creators & publishers
  • ReportingClicks, sales & payouts

FAQs

Affiliate Marketing FAQs

How does affiliate marketing work?

Partners such as bloggers, creators and websites promote your products with a unique link and earn a commission only when they bring a sale or lead.

Is affiliate marketing suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Because you pay for results, it is a low-risk way for small businesses and new brands to reach new customers.

How do you find affiliate partners?

We research and contact bloggers, creators, review sites and niche websites whose audiences match your customers.

How much commission should I offer?

It depends on your margins and industry. We recommend a commission that attracts good partners while keeping your sales profitable.

How are affiliate sales tracked?

Each partner gets tracking links or codes, and the affiliate platform records clicks, sales and commissions automatically.

Which affiliate platform will you use?

We set up a platform that suits your website — for example a Shopify or WordPress affiliate app, or an affiliate network.

How do you prevent fake or fraudulent sales?

We set clear partner rules, monitor traffic and sales quality, and approve commissions only for valid orders.

Can you build an affiliate website for me?

Yes. If you want to earn as an affiliate, we build SEO-friendly affiliate websites and content.

How long does it take to see results?

Programmes usually take a few months to build a strong group of active partners. Results then grow as partners create content.
