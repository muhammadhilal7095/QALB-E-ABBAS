What Is WhatsApp Automation?

WhatsApp Automation uses smart bots and automated workflows to manage customer communication, answer frequently asked questions, share business information, and support customer follow-ups. It helps businesses deliver faster responses and provide a smoother customer experience.

Follow-up messages are only sent with appropriate customer consent, using supported WhatsApp Business tools.
