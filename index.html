Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Should I enroll in Qalb-E-Abbas University or hire your agency for full management?

If you have available capital but lack operational time, our agency services handle full store development, sourcing, legal setup, and digital marketing. If you wish to build internal capabilities and manage your own operations, Qalb-E-Abbas University provides direct mentorship, step-by-step workflows, and live guidance to ensure success.

Why is storing inventory in UK FBA warehouses risky for non-resident sellers?

Foreign entities storing inventory inside UK Amazon FBA or 3PL warehouses are legally required to register for UK VAT from Day 1. The £90,000 domestic UK turnover threshold applies exclusively to UK-established businesses. Selling without Day-1 VAT compliance results in account freezes, inventory seizures, and severe HMRC back-taxes.

What happens if a non-resident US LLC fails to file IRS Form 5472?

Failure to submit Form 5472 alongside Form 1120 pro-forma by the tax deadline triggers an automated $25,000 penalty per tax year from the IRS. Our tax team handles annual compliance to ensure your US entity remains in good standing.

Can I register a US trademark myself if I live outside the United States?

No. Under USPTO mandate 37 CFR § 2.11, foreign-domiciled individuals and entities are legally required to be represented by a US-licensed attorney admitted to a state bar. Self-represented foreign applications are automatically rejected.

Which ad platform should I start with for a new Shopify store?

For novel or visual impulse items, start with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or TikTok Ads to drive top-of-funnel discovery. For products with existing search demand or problem-solving utilities, launch with Google Search and Shopping Ads to capture high-intent buyers immediately.
