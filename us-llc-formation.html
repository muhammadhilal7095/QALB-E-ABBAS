Overview

0% Federal Tax Doesn't Mean Zero Filing

For non-US residents, simply forming an LLC with a cheap online agent without understanding IRS tax obligations leads to severe legal consequences. Single-member US LLCs owned by foreign residents operate as pass-through entities, meaning you owe 0% US federal income tax if you have no US physical presence or Effectively Connected Income (ECI).

Foreign owners of US LLCs often fall into the trap of assuming 0% tax means zero tax filing. We prepare and file your annual Form 5472 and Form 1120 documentation to guarantee complete IRS compliance and protect your entity against automated financial fines.

The $25,000 IRS Form 5472 Penalty

Foreign-owned US LLCs are legally mandated to submit IRS Form 5472 along with Form 1120 pro-forma annually. Missing or late filings trigger an automated $25,000 penalty per year from the IRS.

Our legal and corporate tax team manages your US LLC creation from start to finish — official state filings, compliant virtual business addresses with mail scanning, EIN procurement, US business bank account setup support, and annual IRS tax compliance.

FAQs

US LLC FAQs

Do non-US residents pay federal taxes on US LLC income?

Single-member US LLCs owned by foreign residents pay 0% US federal income tax provided they have no physical presence (employees, physical offices) or Effectively Connected Income (ECI) in the US. However, filing IRS Form 5472 is mandatory.

What is the penalty for late filing of IRS Form 5472?

The IRS levies an automated penalty of $25,000 for each tax year that Form 5472 is filed late or incorrectly.

Can I register a US trademark myself if I live outside the United States?

No. Under USPTO mandate 37 CFR § 2.11, foreign-domiciled individuals and entities are legally required to be represented by a US-licensed attorney admitted to a state bar. Self-represented foreign applications are automatically rejected.
