Introduction

What is Instagram DM automation?

Instagram DM automation connects a chatbot to your Instagram professional account so every direct message, story reply and keyword comment gets an instant, personalised response. It answers common questions, shares product links and prices, and collects contact details automatically.

The most popular flow is comment-to-DM: when someone comments a keyword such as “PRICE” or “LINK” on your post or Reel, they receive a DM with the details straight away. Your audience gets what they asked for, your posts get more engagement, and your team gets warm leads.

FAQs

Instagram Automation FAQs

What can Instagram automation do?

It replies to DMs instantly, sends messages when people comment a keyword, answers common questions, shares product links and collects leads.

Do I need an Instagram business account?

Yes. Automation works with Instagram professional accounts (Business or Creator). We help you switch if needed.

Is Instagram automation safe for my account?

Yes. We use Meta's official messaging tools and never use fake likes, follows or spam bots that put accounts at risk.

Can it send a DM when someone comments on my post?

Yes. For example, when someone comments “PRICE”, they automatically receive a DM with the price and a link.

Can it reply to story replies and mentions?

Yes. Story replies and mentions can trigger automatic, personalised responses.

Can my team take over a conversation?

Yes. The bot hands the chat to a team member whenever a customer needs personal help.

Can it collect customer emails and phone numbers?

Yes. Leads can be sent to your email, Google Sheets or CRM so your team can follow up.

Can it work together with Facebook automation?

Yes. We can set up Instagram and Facebook automation together so customers get consistent answers on both.

How long does setup take?

Simple automations take a few days. Larger flows with integrations take longer — we confirm the timeline before we start.
