Securing a registered trademark is essential to protecting your product lines, preventing brand hijacking on Amazon, and building long-term equity in your business. However, trademark laws are territorial—holding a UK trademark offers zero legal protection in the United States. Furthermore, under USPTO rule 37 CFR § 2.11, foreign-domiciled entities are legally required to be represented by a licensed US attorney to register a trademark in America.

Qalb-E-Abbas provides complete end-to-end trademark registration services in both the US and UK. We perform comprehensive conflict searches, draft precise class descriptions, and manage filings directly through accredited state bar attorneys.
