Qalb-E-Abbas University: FAQs

Do I need any experience to join?

No. The Basic / Level 1 programme starts from the foundations: how marketplaces work, their rules, accounts, costs and margins. If you already sell, the free assessment helps us place you at the right level.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Classes are live and online, so you can ask questions and get answers in real time. Practical tasks between classes help you apply each step to your own business.

Which language are the classes taught in?

Classes are taught in English and Urdu, so you can learn in the language you're most comfortable with.

How long is each programme and what does it cost?

Every programme is 24 live classes of one hour, five days a week, so about five weeks in total. Course fees: Amazon $650, Shopify $250, and eBay, TikTok Shop, Walmart, Etsy and Digital Marketing (Meta, TikTok & Google Ads) $300 each.

Which programme should I start with?

Take the free assessment first. It shows where you are today and which programme fits your goals, whether that's Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, Walmart, Etsy, Shopify or paid advertising.

Can I join from outside Pakistan or the UK?

Yes. All classes are online, so you can join from anywhere with a stable internet connection.

Do I get personal support?

Yes. Every programme includes 1-to-1 guidance from our team, so you get help with your own account, products and plan, not just the general lessons.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or computer, a stable internet connection and time for the practical tasks. We tell you before you start if a platform account or budget is needed for a step.

How do I enrol?

Press "Enrol now" on the course you want, add your details and class preferences, and pay the course fee securely online. We then confirm your class schedule and start date by email. Not sure which course fits? Take the free assessment or book a consultation first.

Is there free learning before I join a programme?

Yes. The free assessment is available to everyone, and our Qalb-E-Abbas University YouTube channel shares free lessons to help you get started.
