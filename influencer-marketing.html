Introduction

What is influencer marketing?

Influencer marketing partners your brand with content creators whose audiences trust their recommendations. Through reviews, tutorials, unboxings and sponsored posts, creators introduce your products to the exact people you want to reach.

The key is choosing the right creators — not just the biggest. We vet creators for real engagement, audience fit and brand safety, manage briefs and contracts, and track every campaign with discount codes and links so you know what it delivered.

FAQs

Influencer Marketing FAQs

How do you choose the right influencers?

We look at audience location, interests, engagement quality and past content — not just follower numbers — to find creators who fit your brand.

Should I work with micro or large influencers?

Micro-influencers often have more trusted, engaged audiences at a lower cost, while larger influencers bring wider reach. Many campaigns use a mix.

Which platforms do you cover?

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.

Are influencer fees included in your price?

No. Our fee covers strategy, outreach and campaign management. Influencer fees or free products are agreed separately with each creator.

Can I use the influencer's content in my ads?

Yes, if usage rights are agreed. We include content rights in the agreement so you can reuse posts and UGC in your ads.

How do you measure campaign results?

We use tracking links, discount codes and platform data to measure reach, engagement, clicks and sales.

Do influencer posts need to be labelled as ads?

Yes. Paid or gifted posts must be clearly labelled, for example with #ad. We make sure every campaign follows the advertising rules.

Can you run product gifting campaigns?

Yes. We send products to suitable creators for honest reviews and content.

How long does a campaign take?

Most campaigns take a few weeks from planning and creator selection to content going live.
