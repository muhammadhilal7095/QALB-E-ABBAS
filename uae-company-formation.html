Overview

Choose the Right Setup from the Start

One of the first decisions is whether to set up on the mainland or in a free zone. The right choice depends on your business activity, where your customers are, and how you plan to operate. We explain the options clearly before any application is made.

From trade name and licensing to office requirements, banking and tax registration, our team guides you through each step in the right order.

Requirements, fees and timelines vary by emirate, jurisdiction and business activity. We confirm the details for your situation during your consultation.
