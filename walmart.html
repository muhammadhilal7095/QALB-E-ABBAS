Introduction

What is Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart Marketplace lets approved third-party sellers sell on walmart.com, one of the most visited retail websites in the United States. Competition is lower than on Amazon, and Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) can store and ship your products for you.

Walmart is selective: it approves established businesses with a proper company setup, valid GS1 barcodes and a good marketplace track record. Once approved, strong listing quality, competitive pricing and fast delivery are what drive sales. We prepare your application and manage your store.

FAQs

Walmart Frequently Asked Questions

Who can sell on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart approves registered businesses — usually with a US company and EIN, a marketplace sales history and products with valid GS1 barcodes. We help you prepare a strong application.

Can I sell on Walmart from outside the US?

International sellers can apply in many cases, but a US LLC, US bank account and US fulfilment (WFS or a 3PL) make approval and selling much easier.

How long does Walmart approval take?

Usually a few days to a few weeks after you submit a complete application. Applications with a US company, EIN, marketplace history and valid GS1 barcodes tend to be approved faster.

Do I need GS1 barcodes for Walmart?

Yes. Walmart checks that your UPC/GTIN barcodes are registered with GS1. Barcodes bought from resellers are often rejected.

What is Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS)?

WFS stores your products in Walmart's warehouses and ships orders with fast delivery, which improves your listings' visibility. We set up and manage WFS inbound shipments.

What is the Walmart Listing Quality score?

It shows how complete and competitive your listings are — content, images, price, shipping speed and reviews. A higher score helps visibility and your chance of winning the Buy Box. Our Walmart SEO service improves it.

How does Walmart advertising work?

Walmart Sponsored Search shows your products at the top of search results. We set up and optimise campaigns so ad spend turns into profitable sales.

What is Walmart live streaming / live shopping?

Walmart runs live shopping events where brands demo products to shoppers in real time. We help you plan the event, prepare content and get stock ready.

My Walmart account is suspended — can you help?

Yes. We review the reason, fix performance or policy issues and prepare an appeal to restore your account.
