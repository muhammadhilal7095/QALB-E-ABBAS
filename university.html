University Curriculum Modules

Four modules that take you from foundations to legal compliance, Amazon mastery and direct-to-consumer growth.

  1. Module 01

    E-Commerce Foundations & Progression Mapping

    Learn the exact order to launch channels to preserve capital and minimize operational risk.

    1. eBay
    2. Etsy
    3. TikTok Shop
    4. Walmart
    5. Shopify
    6. Amazon
  2. Module 02

    US & UK Corporate Entity Legal Compliance

    Mastering US LLC pass-through tax rules, IRS Form 5472 documentation, UK LTD setup, and HMRC Day-1 VAT mandates for non-residents.

  3. Module 03

    Amazon FBA & Private Label Mastery

    Product research frameworks, factory negotiations, FBA prep requirements, Amazon Brand Registry, and advanced PPC bidding strategies.

  4. Module 04

    Direct-to-Consumer Shopify & Digital Marketing

    Custom Shopify store builds, CRO fundamentals, Meta Advantage+ ads, Google Shopping campaigns, and automated retention flows.
