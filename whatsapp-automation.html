Introduction

What is WhatsApp Business automation?

WhatsApp Business automation connects a chatbot to your business number on the WhatsApp Business Platform (API). It answers customers instantly, shares product and service details, books appointments and sends order updates — in the app your customers already use every day.

Unlike the free WhatsApp Business app, the Business Platform supports chatbots, several team members on one number, integrations with your website, store or CRM, and approved message templates for notifications and opt-in offers. We handle the setup, verification and templates for you.

FAQs

WhatsApp Automation FAQs

What is WhatsApp automation?

A chatbot connected to your WhatsApp business number that replies instantly, answers questions, shares product details, books appointments and sends order updates.

What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business Platform?

The free app suits small teams replying by hand. The WhatsApp Business Platform (API) supports chatbots, multiple team members, integrations and automated notifications — this is what we use for automation.

Do I need a new phone number?

You can use a new number or, in many cases, connect your existing business number. We check the best option for you before setup.

Does WhatsApp charge for messages?

Meta charges for some message types, mainly business-initiated messages such as promotions and notifications. Prices depend on the country and message type, and we explain them before you launch.

Can I send promotional broadcasts?

Yes, to customers who have agreed to receive messages, using templates approved by WhatsApp. This keeps your number in good standing.

Can customers book appointments on WhatsApp?

Yes. The bot can show available times, confirm bookings and send reminders.

Can it send order confirmations and delivery updates?

Yes. We can connect your store or system so customers get automatic order and delivery messages.

Can my team take over the chat?

Yes. Conversations can be handed to a team member at any time.

Can it connect to my website, CRM or Shopify store?

Yes. We integrate WhatsApp with your website, CRM, Google Sheets or store so leads and orders flow automatically.
