A Team Built to Help People Grow

Qalb-E-Abbas is a business services and education company based in Manchester, United Kingdom, working with clients around the world.

We bring together specialists in e-commerce, digital marketing, websites, company formation, legal services and taxation, auditing, real estate and education under one roof.

We started with a simple belief: everyone deserves a fair chance to build a better life. Many people have the drive to succeed but lack the knowledge, the right connections or honest advice. We exist to close that gap — by teaching what works, doing the work properly when clients need us, and standing beside them until they are confident on their own.

Whether you need a marketplace store managed for you, a company formed abroad or structured mentorship through Qalb-E-Abbas University, you get one joined-up team that puts your long-term success first.
