Introduction

Why non-residents need specialist tax filing

Running a US LLC or UK limited company from abroad comes with filing obligations that many founders don't know about until a penalty arrives. Foreign-owned US LLCs must file IRS Form 5472 with a pro-forma Form 1120 every year — even with zero US tax due — or face a $25,000 penalty.

UK companies must file annual accounts with Companies House and a Corporation Tax return (CT600) with HMRC, plus VAT returns once registered. We prepare and submit every filing on time, at fixed prices, so you can focus on growing your business.

FAQs

Tax Filing Frequently Asked Questions

Does my foreign-owned US LLC need to file taxes?

Yes. A single-member US LLC owned by a non-resident must file IRS Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year — even if it made no sales.

When is the US Form 5472 deadline?

For LLCs using the calendar year it is due by 15 April. You can get a six-month extension to 15 October by filing Form 7004 on time.

What happens if I don't file Form 5472?

The IRS can charge a $25,000 penalty for each year the form is filed late, incorrectly or not at all, so filing on time is essential.

What does “+ state fees” mean on the US tax filing price?

Our $200 fee covers preparing and filing your federal forms. Annual reports or franchise taxes charged by your LLC's state are paid to the state and depend on where the LLC is registered.

What does the Complete UK Accounting Package include?

For £180 a year we prepare and submit your annual accounts to Companies House and your Corporation Tax return (CT600) to HMRC, with compliance support throughout the year.

When are UK accounts and Corporation Tax due?

Annual accounts are due at Companies House 9 months after your financial year end. Corporation Tax is usually payable 9 months and 1 day after the year end, and the tax return is due within 12 months.

My company had no income — do I still need to file?

Yes. A US LLC still files Form 5472 and pro forma 1120, and a UK company still files annual accounts and a confirmation statement with Companies House, even when it has not traded.

What records do I need to send you?

Bank statements, marketplace or payment platform sales reports, invoices and receipts for expenses, and copies of any previous filings. We send you a simple checklist.

Can you help with late or missed filings?

Yes. We can prepare catch-up filings for past years. Penalties may already apply, so acting quickly is the best way to limit them.
