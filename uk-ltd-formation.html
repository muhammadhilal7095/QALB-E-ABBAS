UK LTD Formation Services: Complete Company Setup & HMRC Tax Compliance for Foreign Founders

Establishing a UK Limited company provides foreign entrepreneurs with direct access to European payment processors, trusted banking infrastructure, and global marketplaces. However, operating a UK LTD requires strict adherence to Companies House regulations and HMRC tax mandates. A major operational risk for non-resident sellers storing inventory in UK warehouses (such as Amazon FBA) is missing the Day-1 UK VAT Mandate. Foreign businesses storing inventory inside the UK do not qualify for the £90,000 domestic registration threshold and must register for VAT immediately.

Qalb-E-Abbas provides a seamless UK incorporation package. We handle Companies House filings, unique suite business addresses with mail forwarding, HMRC ID verification support, UK business banking assistance, and complete ongoing annual accounting.
