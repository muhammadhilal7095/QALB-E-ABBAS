Introduction

What is Shopify?

Shopify is the world's leading platform for building your own online store. Unlike a marketplace, your Shopify store is your brand: you own the customer relationship, the data and the design, with no marketplace fees per sale beyond payment processing.

The trade-off is traffic — a new store gets no visitors on its own. That's why a great Shopify store needs both a fast, high-converting design and steady advertising on Meta, TikTok and Google. We build your store and run the ads that bring customers to it.

  • OwnershipYour brand, customers and data
  • PaymentsShopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal and more
  • TrafficMeta, TikTok and Google ads plus SEO
  • Key skillsConversion, ads and email marketing

FAQs

Shopify Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a Shopify store?

A standard store takes about 1–2 weeks once we have your products, logo and content. Larger catalogs or custom designs take longer.

What does the $250 Shopify website development include?

Theme setup and design, homepage and product pages, collections, payments, shipping settings, essential apps and a mobile-friendly checkout.

Do I need a company to open a Shopify store?

Not always, but a registered business and a business bank account make payments, payouts and ad accounts much easier — especially for Shopify Payments.

Which payment methods can my store accept?

Shopify Payments is available in many countries; where it isn't, you can use PayPal and other supported payment providers. We set up the options that work for your country and business.

What do Meta ads management services include?

Ad handling and campaign setup, marketing strategy, post boosting, and content management for Facebook and Instagram, with monthly reporting. The $150 / month fee does not include your ad spend.

Is ad spend included in the monthly ads price?

No. The monthly fee covers our management. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta, TikTok or Google.

Which ads should I start with — Meta, TikTok or Google?

For new, visual or impulse products start with Meta or TikTok. If people already search for your product, start with Google Search and Shopping. We recommend the right mix for your budget.

Can you help my store rank on Google?

Yes. Our Shopify SEO service covers technical SEO, page speed, product and collection pages, and content to help your store rank.

Do you provide support after the store goes live?

Yes. After launch we can help with updates, new products, apps and speed fixes, and our growth, SEO and ads services help you turn visitors into sales.

How do I pay for your Shopify services?

Add the services you need to your cart on this page and pay securely by card. You'll get an email confirmation and our team contacts you within one working day.
