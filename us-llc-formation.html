Introduction

Why form a US LLC as a non-resident?

A US limited liability company (LLC) is the most popular way for international entrepreneurs to sell on Amazon US, accept payments through Stripe and PayPal, open US business bank accounts and build trust with American customers and suppliers.

A foreign-owned single-member LLC with no US trade or business usually owes no US federal income tax — but it must still file IRS Form 5472 with a pro-forma Form 1120 every year. Missing it can trigger a $25,000 penalty. We form your LLC correctly and keep it compliant.

0% Federal Tax Doesn't Mean Zero Filing

Many foreign owners assume a US LLC with no US tax means no paperwork. The IRS disagrees — and the penalty for getting it wrong is severe.

Foreign-owned single-member LLCs are treated as pass-through entities, so they usually owe no US federal income tax without US effectively connected income. FAQs

US LLC Formation FAQs

Can a non-US resident form a US LLC?

Yes. You don't need US citizenship, residence or a visa. You need a registered agent and an address in the state where you form the LLC — both are included in our packages.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

Wyoming, Delaware and New Mexico are popular with non-residents because of low fees and simple compliance. The best choice depends on where you sell and your plans, so we recommend a state during your consultation.

Do I need an SSN or ITIN to get an EIN?

No. Non-residents without an SSN or ITIN can get an EIN by applying to the IRS with Form SS-4. We prepare and submit it for you.

How long does US LLC formation take?

State filing usually takes from a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on the state. The EIN follows after that; the Advanced package includes fast-track EIN procurement.

Can I open a US bank account without visiting the US?

Several US banks and fintech providers, such as Mercury and Relay, accept non-resident LLC owners remotely, subject to their own checks. We help you prepare a strong application; approval is decided by the bank.

What is the difference between the Basic and Advanced packages?

Basic (from $585 total, including your state's filing fee) covers state filing, a registered agent for one year, a virtual US address, EIN procurement and bank signup guidance. Advanced (from $685 total) adds a commercial suite address, expedited EIN, full bank setup assistance and one year of IRS Form 5472 & 1120 filing. Your state's filing fee is added on top of either package — choose your state on this page to see the exact total.

Do non-US residents pay federal taxes on US LLC income?

Single-member US LLCs owned by foreign residents pay 0% US federal income tax provided they have no physical presence (employees, physical offices) or Effectively Connected Income (ECI) in the US. However, filing IRS Form 5472 is mandatory.

What is the penalty for late filing of IRS Form 5472?

The IRS levies an automated penalty of $25,000 for each tax year that Form 5472 is filed late or incorrectly.

What yearly fees does a US LLC have?

Most states charge an annual report fee or franchise tax, and you need to renew your registered agent each year. The amount depends on the state — we tell you exactly what applies before you form.

Can I register a US trademark myself if I live outside the United States?

No. Under USPTO mandate 37 CFR § 2.11, foreign-domiciled individuals and entities are legally required to be represented by a US-licensed attorney admitted to a state bar. Self-represented foreign applications are automatically rejected.
