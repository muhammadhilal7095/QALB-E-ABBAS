Why register a company in Pakistan?

A registered company gives your business a separate legal identity, limited liability and the trust that banks, suppliers, marketplaces and government buyers expect. It also makes it easier to open a business bank account and bring in partners or investors.

Companies in Pakistan are registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) under the Companies Act 2017. We help you choose the right structure, prepare your documents, complete SECP incorporation and register with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR), so your company is ready to trade.

The right structure depends on your owners, plans and investors. Here's a quick guide.

  • Private Limited (Pvt) LtdTwo or more shareholders, limited liability and the most common choice for growing businesses.
  • Single Member Company (SMC-Pvt)One owner with limited liability, plus a nominee as required by law.
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)A flexible option for professionals and partners.

Pakistan Company Formation FAQs

Who registers companies in Pakistan?

Companies are registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) under the Companies Act 2017. Registration is done online through the SECP's eServices portal.

Which company type should I choose?

A Private Limited company suits most businesses with two or more owners. A Single Member Company (SMC-Pvt) suits one owner, and an LLP suits professional partnerships. We recommend the right one for your plans.

Can overseas Pakistanis register a company in Pakistan?

Yes. Overseas Pakistanis can be shareholders and directors using their NICOP or passport, and most of the process can be completed online from abroad.

Can foreign nationals own a company in Pakistan?

Yes. Foreign investors can own shares in most sectors. Foreign shareholders and directors usually need security clearance, which can add time, so we plan for it from the start.

How long does company registration take?

Once the name is approved and documents are complete, SECP incorporation is often done within a few working days. Cases needing security clearance take longer.

What documents do I need?

Usually CNIC or NICOP copies (or passports for foreign nationals), contact details for each director and shareholder, the company's registered address and its business activity. We give you a checklist for your case.

Do I get an NTN after registration?

Yes. Your company needs a National Tax Number with the Federal Board of Revenue. We make sure it is issued and set up correctly after incorporation.

Do I need to register for sales tax?

Only if your business needs it, for example manufacturers, importers or certain service providers. Services may fall under provincial sales tax authorities. We advise you on what applies.

What are the yearly obligations of a Pakistan company?

Companies file an annual return with the SECP, keep proper books, prepare financial statements and file a yearly income tax return with FBR. We can handle these for you.

Can you help open a business bank account?

Yes. We prepare the company documents banks ask for and guide you through the business bank account application.
