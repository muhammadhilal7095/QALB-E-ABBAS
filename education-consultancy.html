FAQs

Study in Malta FAQs

Why study in Malta?

Malta is an English-speaking EU country with recognised qualifications, a safe environment and a wide range of bachelor's, master's, diploma and English language courses.

Who can apply through Qalb-E-Abbas?

Students who have completed the qualifications needed for their chosen level — for example, college or intermediate for a bachelor's degree, or a bachelor's degree for a master's.

Do I need IELTS to study in Malta?

Most institutions ask for proof of English, such as IELTS. Some accept alternatives or offer English language courses first. We check the requirement for each course you apply to.

What documents will I need?

Usually your passport, academic certificates and transcripts, proof of English, a CV, a statement of purpose and proof of funds. We give you a checklist for your chosen course.

When are the main intakes?

Most programmes start in September or October, and some also start in February. Applying early gives more time for admission and visa processing.

Can you guarantee admission or a student visa?

No. Admission is decided by the institution and visas by the Maltese authorities. We make sure your application is complete and well prepared to give you the best chance.

Can I work while studying in Malta?

Non-EU students may be able to work limited part-time hours with the right permit. The rules can change, so we explain the current conditions during your consultation.

What does the application processing fee cover?

Our support with course and institution selection, application preparation, admission follow-up and student visa preparation. Tuition, visa and other third-party fees are paid separately.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We share scholarship and funding information that matches your profile and course.
