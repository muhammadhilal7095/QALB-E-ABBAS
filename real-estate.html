Introduction

Why invest in real estate in Pakistan?

Property is one of the most popular ways Pakistani families — at home and abroad — build long-term wealth. Growing cities such as Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Karachi have steady demand for houses, plots, apartments and commercial space.

But buying property in Pakistan needs care. Housing society approvals, title documents (fard, registry and intiqal / mutation), plot and file verification, FBR taxes and transfer procedures all differ by city and authority. We guide you step by step — and for overseas Pakistanis, we coordinate the process so you can buy, sell or rent without travelling every time.

Real Estate in Pakistan — FAQs

Can overseas Pakistanis buy property in Pakistan?

Yes. Overseas Pakistanis can buy, sell and rent property in Pakistan. Many buy remotely with video tours and an attested power of attorney given to a trusted representative. We coordinate the process and keep you updated.

Which cities do you cover?

We support property searches and transactions in major cities such as Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Karachi. Tell us your preferred city or housing society and we'll confirm how we can help.

How do I check that a property's documents are genuine?

Ownership should be confirmed against official land records and property documents such as the fard, registry and intiqal (mutation), plus the society's allotment or transfer letters. We arrange these checks with an independent lawyer before you pay anything.

How can I check if a housing society is approved?

Housing society approval and NOC status can be checked with the relevant development authority for that area. We verify the society's status as part of our due diligence before you buy.

What taxes apply when buying or selling property?

Buyers and sellers usually pay stamp duty, registration or transfer fees and FBR withholding taxes. Withholding tax rates are higher for non-filers, so your FBR filer status matters. Rates change, so we explain the current costs before you commit.

Can I buy property in Pakistan without travelling?

Often, yes. With an attested power of attorney, a trusted representative can complete steps on your behalf. We provide video tours, document copies and regular updates throughout the process.

Do you help with selling my property?

Yes. We help with pricing guidance, professional photos, online listings and social media promotion to reach serious buyers, then support you through the agreement and transfer.

Do you offer rental and property management?

Yes. We can help find tenants, prepare tenancy agreements, collect rent and coordinate maintenance — useful if you live abroad or don't have time to manage the property yourself.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide property guidance and coordination. Legal checks, agreements and transfers should be completed with an independent, qualified lawyer, and we work alongside your lawyer.
