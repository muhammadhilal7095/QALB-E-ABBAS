Why file your tax return in Pakistan?

Filing your income tax return puts you on the Federal Board of Revenue's Active Taxpayers List (ATL). Filers pay lower withholding tax on things like property transfers, vehicle registration and bank transactions, and find it easier to get loans, visas and government contracts.

The tax year in Pakistan runs from 1 July to 30 June, and returns are filed online through the FBR's IRIS portal. We register your NTN if you don't have one, collect your income and wealth details, prepare your return and wealth statement, and file them correctly before the deadline.

Pakistan Tax Filing FAQs

Who needs to file an income tax return in Pakistan?

Anyone whose taxable income is above the threshold must file, along with certain people who own property or vehicles, or have business or company registrations. Many people also file voluntarily to become a filer.

What is the Active Taxpayers List (ATL)?

The ATL is FBR's list of people and businesses who have filed their returns. Being on it means you pay lower withholding tax on many transactions, such as property and vehicle purchases.

When is the tax return deadline?

The tax year runs from 1 July to 30 June. For most individuals and AOPs the return is due by 30 September, unless FBR extends it. Companies have their own due dates.

What is a wealth statement?

A wealth statement lists your assets and liabilities at the end of the tax year. Individuals file it with their return, and it must reconcile with your income and spending.

Do I need an NTN to file?

Yes. Individuals use their CNIC number as their NTN after registering on FBR's IRIS portal. Businesses and companies get their own NTN. We handle the registration if needed.

Can overseas Pakistanis file tax returns?

Yes. Overseas Pakistanis can register and file using their NICOP. We check your residency status first, because it affects which income is taxable in Pakistan.

What documents do I need to file?

Usually your salary certificate or business income details, bank statements, withholding tax certificates and details of property, vehicles and other assets.

What happens if I file late?

Late returns can lead to penalties and you may not appear on the ATL until you file, which means higher withholding tax. Filing on time avoids this.

Can you help with sales tax returns?

Yes. We register you for sales tax with FBR or the relevant provincial authority and file your monthly sales tax returns.

I received a notice from FBR. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the notice and we'll review it, prepare a clear reply and handle the follow-up with FBR.
