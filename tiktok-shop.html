What is TikTok Shop?

TikTok Shop lets people buy products directly inside TikTok — from short videos, LIVE streams, creator content and the Shop tab — without leaving the app. For brands and online sellers it has become one of the fastest-growing e-commerce channels in the UK and US, because products are discovered through entertaining content rather than search alone.

FAQs

TikTok Shop Frequently Asked Questions

Who can open a TikTok Shop account?

TikTok Shop is built for sellers registered in the selling country. For TikTok Shop UK or US you'll usually need a UK or US business, local address, bank account and fast local delivery. We help you set this up.

Do I need lots of followers to sell on TikTok Shop?

No. Products can sell through the Shop tab, search and creator affiliate videos even when your own account is new. Regular posting and good creators help you grow faster.

How does TikTok LIVE selling work?

You go live from your TikTok Shop account and pin products to the stream; viewers buy without leaving the LIVE. Planned shows with offers, demos and a good host sell the most.

What are TikTok creator affiliates?

Creators promote your products in their videos and LIVEs and earn a commission on sales. We find creators who suit your products, send samples and manage the programme.

Why was my TikTok Shop product rejected?

Common reasons are restricted categories, missing certificates, poor images or misleading claims. We review the reason and fix the listing or documents.

How fast do I need to ship TikTok Shop orders?

TikTok Shop has strict shipping deadlines, usually 1–3 working days to dispatch. Shipping from abroad rarely meets this, so we plan a local 3PL or Fulfilled by TikTok.

What is Fulfilled by TikTok?

TikTok's own fulfilment service in some markets, including the UK and US. It stores, packs and ships your orders so you meet delivery deadlines. We check whether your products qualify and help you set it up.

What is shop health and violation points?

TikTok tracks late shipments, cancellations, returns and policy breaks. Too many violation points can restrict or deactivate your shop. Our account review shows you what to fix.

Can you reactivate a deactivated TikTok Shop?

Often, yes. We review the deactivation reason and prepare an appeal with a clear corrective action plan.
