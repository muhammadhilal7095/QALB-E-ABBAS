US LLC Formation Services: Complete Non-Resident Corporate Setup & IRS Tax Compliance

Establishing a US Limited Liability Company (LLC) is the gold standard for global entrepreneurs seeking to sell on Amazon US, access US payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal), and build credibility with American suppliers. However, for non-US residents, simply forming an LLC with a cheap online agent without understanding IRS tax obligations leads to severe legal consequences. Single-member US LLCs owned by foreign residents operate as pass-through entities, meaning you owe 0% US federal income tax if you have no US physical presence or Effectively Connected Income (ECI).

However, foreign-owned US LLCs are legally mandated to submit IRS Form 5472 along with Form 1120 pro-forma annually. Missing or late filings trigger an automated $25,000 penalty per year from the IRS. At Qalb-E-Abbas, our legal and corporate tax team manages your US LLC creation from start to finish. We provide official state filings, compliant virtual business addresses with mail scanning, Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) procurement, US business bank account setup support, and annual IRS tax compliance.

US LLC FAQs

Do non-US residents pay federal taxes on US LLC income?

Single-member US LLCs owned by foreign residents pay 0% US federal income tax provided they have no physical presence (employees, physical offices) or Effectively Connected Income (ECI) in the US. However, filing IRS Form 5472 is mandatory.

What is the penalty for late filing of IRS Form 5472?

The IRS levies an automated penalty of $25,000 for each tax year that Form 5472 is filed late or incorrectly.
