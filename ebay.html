FAQs

eBay Frequently Asked Questions

What are eBay MC011 and MC999 restrictions?

They are account restrictions eBay applies when it has concerns about verification, sourcing or policy. MC011 often relates to selling practices or dropshipping; MC999 usually needs identity and supplier verification. We prepare the right evidence and appeal.

Can I open an eBay seller account from Pakistan or another country?

Yes, but eBay checks that your ID, address, phone and bank details match. We help you prepare documents and the right business setup so verification succeeds.

Is dropshipping allowed on eBay?

Buying from another retailer (like Amazon) and having it shipped to your eBay buyer isn't allowed and often leads to MC011 restrictions. Wholesale dropshipping from a supplier is allowed if you meet eBay's delivery standards.

What are eBay selling limits?

New accounts have limits on how many items and how much value you can list each month. Limits increase as you build good sales history and feedback.

How does eBay SEO (Cassini) work?

eBay's search engine ranks listings on relevance, complete item specifics, price, shipping and seller performance. Our SEO audit fixes titles, item specifics and categories.

What is included in the eBay marketing strategy?

A plan for Promoted Listings, markdown sales, volume pricing and store promotions, with a budget and the products to push first.

What is eBay Top Rated Seller status?

It rewards sellers with low defect rates, on-time shipping with tracking and enough sales history. Qualifying listings can get a fee discount and a badge that builds buyer trust. Our account review shows what to improve to reach it.

Can I sell on eBay UK and eBay US from one account?

Yes, eBay lets you list on its other sites, but each market has its own fees, delivery expectations and VAT or sales tax rules. We help you choose where to start and how to ship there.

How long does an eBay reactivation take?

It varies with the reason for suspension. Once the appeal and documents are ready we submit them quickly and follow up until eBay responds.
