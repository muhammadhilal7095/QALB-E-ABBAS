Introduction

What is CRM development?

A CRM (customer relationship management) system keeps all your leads, customers, deals and conversations in one place. Your team always knows who to call, what was discussed and what happens next — so no opportunity slips through the cracks.

Off-the-shelf CRMs often force you to change how you work. We build custom CRM software around your sales process, team structure and reporting needs, and connect it to the tools you already use — from your website forms and WhatsApp to email and accounting.

FAQs

CRM Development FAQs

Why choose a custom CRM over ready-made software?

A custom CRM is built around how your business works, with only the features you need, no per-user subscription limits and room to grow.

How long does it take to build a CRM?

A focused CRM can be ready in a few weeks; larger systems with many integrations take longer. We confirm the timeline after requirement analysis.

Can the CRM connect to my website, WhatsApp and email?

Yes. We integrate website forms, WhatsApp, email and other tools so leads arrive in the CRM automatically.

Can I control what each staff member sees?

Yes. Role-based access lets you decide who can view, edit or export data.

Can I access the CRM from anywhere?

Yes. We build cloud-based CRMs that work in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone.

Can you move my existing data into the new CRM?

Yes. We import your customers, leads and history from spreadsheets or your current system.

Is my data secure?

We use secure hosting, encrypted connections, user permissions and regular backups to protect your data.

Can the CRM automate follow-ups and tasks?

Yes. Workflow automation can assign leads, send reminders and follow-up messages, and update deal stages.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes. We train your team and provide maintenance and improvements after launch.
