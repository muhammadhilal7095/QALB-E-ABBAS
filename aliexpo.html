Ali Expo FAQs

What is Ali Expo dropshipping?

You sell products in your own online store, and when a customer orders, the supplier ships the item directly to your customer. You don't need to buy or store stock upfront.

Can I sell my own products on Ali Expo?

Yes. We help you set up your Ali Expo seller account, prepare your documents and list your products.

Which platform is best for a dropshipping store?

Most of our clients use Shopify, connected to their suppliers with an order automation app. Products can also be sold on TikTok Shop, eBay or your own website.

How do you choose reliable suppliers?

We compare store ratings, reviews, order history, delivery times and communication, and order samples for key products before you list them.

How long does shipping take?

It depends on the supplier, the shipping method and the destination. We prioritise suppliers with fast, tracked delivery to your customers' country.

How much money do I need to start?

You can start with a small budget: a store plan, a few apps, sample orders and an advertising budget. We give you a clear cost breakdown during your consultation.

Do you automate orders and tracking?

Yes. We set up order automation so orders are placed with your suppliers in a few clicks, and tracking numbers and stock levels sync back to your store automatically.

Can you help me buy products in bulk?

Yes. We help you buy in bulk for Amazon FBA, Daraz or your own shop, and can arrange branded packaging and shipping to your warehouse.

How do I handle returns and refunds?

We set up clear store policies and show you how to handle returns, refunds and supplier disputes fairly.

Do you offer an Ali Expo course?

Yes. Qalb-E-Abbas University runs a live Ali Expo dropshipping programme: 24 live classes, one hour a day, five days a week.
