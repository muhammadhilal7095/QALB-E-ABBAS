Introduction

What is digital marketing and paid advertising?

Digital marketing uses online channels — search engines, social media, video and email — to reach the people most likely to buy from you. Paid advertising (PPC) puts your business in front of those people instantly, with precise targeting and measurable results.

We plan, launch and manage campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn. Every campaign starts with proper conversion tracking, so budget moves to the ads that bring leads and sales — and away from the ones that don't.

FAQs

Digital Marketing Frequently Asked Questions

Which advertising platform is best for my business?

It depends on your product and customers. Meta and TikTok are great for visual and impulse products, while Google captures people already searching. We recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Is ad spend included in the monthly price?

No. The monthly fee covers our management. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta, TikTok or Google.

How much should I spend on ads?

Start with a test budget you are comfortable with. Once we see which ads and audiences work, we scale the budget behind the winners.

How soon will I see results?

Ads start delivering as soon as they're approved, but it usually takes 2–4 weeks of testing and optimisation to find the best-performing campaigns.

Do you create the ad creatives?

Yes. Creative development is part of our process — we plan ad images, videos and copy that suit each platform.

Will I own my ad accounts?

Yes. Ad accounts and business managers stay in your name, and we work through the access you give us.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes. We set up the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, TikTok Pixel and Google tags so every sale and lead is measured.

Can you run ads for my Shopify or e-commerce store?

Yes. E-commerce advertising, retargeting and catalogue ads are a core part of our service.

How do you report results?

You receive regular reports with spend, sales or leads, cost per result and what we are changing next.

How do I pay for your marketing services?

Add the services you need to your cart on this page and pay securely by card. You'll get an email confirmation and our team contacts you within one working day.
