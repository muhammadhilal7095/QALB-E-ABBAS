Introduction

What is Facebook Messenger automation?

Facebook Messenger automation connects a chatbot to your Facebook page so every message gets an answer the moment it arrives. The bot greets customers, answers common questions, shares prices and product details, and collects names, phone numbers and emails — day and night.

It also works with your posts and ads. When someone comments on a post or clicks a Click-to-Messenger ad, the bot can start a private conversation and guide them to a sale or booking. When a customer needs a real person, the chat is handed straight to your team.

FAQs

Facebook Automation FAQs

What can a Facebook page automation bot do?

It replies to Messenger messages instantly, answers common questions, shares product and service details, collects leads and follows up with customers — all from your Facebook page.

Is Facebook automation allowed by Meta?

Yes. We build on Meta's official Messenger tools and follow its messaging policies, so your page stays safe.

Will the bot reply 24/7?

Yes. Customers get an instant answer at any time, even outside business hours.

Can a real person take over the conversation?

Yes. The bot can hand the chat to your team when a customer needs personal help or is ready to buy.

Can it reply to comments on my posts?

Yes. It can reply to comments automatically and send a private message to people who comment, turning comments into conversations.

What is Meta's 24-hour messaging window?

Businesses can freely reply to a customer within 24 hours of their last message. After that, only limited message types are allowed. We design your flows to work within these rules.

Can the bot collect leads and send them to my team?

Yes. It can collect names, phone numbers and emails and send them to your email, Google Sheets or CRM.

Can the bot reply in Urdu or other languages?

Yes. Conversation flows can be written in English, Urdu or any language your customers use.

What do I need to get started?

A Facebook page and admin access, plus the questions customers usually ask. We handle the setup, testing and launch.
