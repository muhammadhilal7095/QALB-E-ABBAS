FAQs

Website Development FAQs

How long does it take to build a website?

A business website usually takes 1–3 weeks. E-commerce and custom websites take longer depending on pages and features.

Which platform will you use?

We recommend WordPress, Shopify or a custom build depending on your goals, budget and how you want to manage the site.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Is the website SEO-friendly?

Yes. We build with clean structure, fast loading, meta tags and mobile optimisation so your site is ready for search engines.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We help you choose and set up your domain, hosting and business email, all registered in your name.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We build on easy-to-use systems and show you how to edit text, images and products.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We redesign outdated websites to improve their look, speed and results.

Can you add online payments or booking?

Yes. We can add payment gateways, booking and appointment systems, memberships and other features.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. We provide ongoing support for updates, backups, security and changes.
