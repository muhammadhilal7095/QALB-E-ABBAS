FAQs

Malta Company Formation FAQs

Why set up a company in Malta?

Malta is an EU member state with English as an official language, giving access to the EU single market, a well-regulated business environment and a tax system that can be efficient for international owners.

Do I need to live in Malta to set up a company there?

Requirements depend on the company structure and business activity. We review your situation during your consultation and explain what applies to you.

Which type of company is most common in Malta?

Most founders choose a private limited liability company (Ltd). It separates your personal assets from the business and suits trading, e-commerce and services companies.

What is the minimum share capital?

A Maltese private limited company needs a minimum share capital of €1,165, and at least 20% of it must be paid up when the company is registered.

How long does it take to register a Malta company?

Once documents, due diligence and the share capital deposit are complete, registration with the Malta Business Registry is usually quick. Opening the bank account often takes longer, so we start that process early.

Do I need a registered office in Malta?

Yes. Every Maltese company must have a registered office address in Malta. We guide you on suitable options.

How is a Malta company taxed?

The headline corporate tax rate is 35%, but Malta's tax refund system can significantly reduce the overall tax for shareholders on distributed profits. Whether you qualify depends on your situation, so we explain it during your consultation.

Do I need to register for VAT in Malta?

It depends on your business activity and turnover, and on whether you sell to other EU countries. We check what applies and guide you through registration if you need it.

What ongoing compliance does a Malta company have?

Each year the company files an annual return with the Malta Business Registry, prepares audited financial statements and submits a tax return. We help you stay on top of every deadline.

Can you help with tax after the company is registered?

Yes. We offer business tax filing and compliance support in Malta as part of our Taxation Services.
