Introduction

Why form a UK limited company?

A UK private limited company (LTD) gives founders from anywhere in the world a trusted business base. It opens access to UK and European customers, marketplaces like Amazon UK, payment processors such as Stripe and PayPal, and reliable UK business banking.

Directors and shareholders don't need to live in the UK, but every company needs a UK registered office, directors who complete Companies House identity verification, and ongoing filings with Companies House and HMRC. We handle the formation and keep your company compliant afterwards.

FAQs

UK Company Formation FAQs

Can a non-UK resident form a UK limited company?

Yes. Directors and shareholders can live anywhere in the world. You need a UK registered office address, at least one director and must complete Companies House identity verification — we help with each step.

How long does UK company registration take?

Once your details and identity verification are ready, Companies House usually registers the company within a few working days. We prepare everything in advance so the application isn't delayed.

Can I use my home address abroad as the registered office?

No. A UK company's registered office must be in the UK. Both our Basic and Advanced plans include a UK registered office and business address.

What is the difference between the Basic and Advanced plans?

Both include Companies House registration, company documents, a registered office address and HMRC ID verification assistance. The Advanced plan adds mail forwarding, a business email and domain for one year, and dedicated help until your business bank account is open.

Can I open a UK business bank account as a non-resident?

It is possible, but traditional high-street banks often require UK residence. Many overseas founders use regulated online business accounts. We guide you through the options and the application.

Do I need to register for UK VAT?

UK-based businesses must register once taxable turnover goes over £90,000. Overseas businesses selling goods stored in the UK must register from the first sale. Our VAT & Tax Filing plan covers this.

Why is storing inventory in UK FBA warehouses risky for non-resident sellers?

Foreign entities storing inventory inside UK Amazon FBA or 3PL warehouses are legally required to register for UK VAT from Day 1. The £90,000 domestic UK turnover threshold applies exclusively to UK-established businesses. Selling without Day-1 VAT compliance results in account freezes, inventory seizures, and severe HMRC back-taxes.

How much Corporation Tax will my UK company pay?

Corporation Tax is 19% on profits up to £50,000 and 25% on profits over £250,000, with marginal relief in between. Rates can change, so we confirm the current rates when we prepare your return.

What do I need to file every year?

Each year the company files a confirmation statement and annual accounts with Companies House, and a Corporation Tax return with HMRC. Late filing brings automatic penalties.

What does the annual accounting package include?

Our Complete UK Accounting Package (£180 / year — just £18.33/month) covers annual accounts preparation and submission to Companies House, Corporation Tax Return filing with HMRC, and full ongoing compliance management throughout the year.
