Overview

What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based marketing strategy in which businesses partner with affiliates who promote their products or services in exchange for a commission on qualifying sales, leads, or other agreed actions.

It allows businesses to expand their reach, connect with new audiences, and reward partners based on measurable results.

Why Choose Affiliate Marketing?

  • Expand your reach through partner networks.
  • Attract relevant visitors to your website.
  • Create additional sales opportunities.
  • Build partnerships with publishers and creators.
  • Track measurable campaign performance.
  • Reward affiliates for qualifying results.
  • Scale marketing efforts according to your business goals.
