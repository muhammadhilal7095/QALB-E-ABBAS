Introduction

What is SEO and why does it matter?

Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the process of improving your website so it ranks higher in Google's organic search results. Better rankings put your business in front of people who are already searching for your products and services — without paying for every click.

Good SEO combines the right keywords, helpful content, a fast and crawlable website and trusted links from other sites. We handle each part with a clear plan, then track rankings, traffic and enquiries so you can see exactly what your investment delivers.

FAQs

SEO Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites start to see movement within 3–6 months. Competitive keywords and new websites can take longer, which is why we track progress from the start.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings, because Google controls its results. We use proven methods that improve your visibility and report the results clearly.

What is the difference between on-page, off-page and technical SEO?

On-page SEO improves your content, titles and keywords. Off-page SEO builds your site's authority with quality links and mentions. Technical SEO fixes speed, indexing and site structure so Google can crawl you properly.

Do you offer local SEO?

Yes. We optimise your Google Business Profile, local pages and listings so you appear in local and map searches.

Do you work with Shopify and WordPress websites?

Yes. We optimise Shopify, WordPress and custom websites, including product and collection pages for online stores.

Which access will you need?

Usually access to your website's admin, Google Search Console and Google Analytics. Everything stays in your name and you can remove our access at any time.

Are your SEO methods safe?

Yes. We follow Google's guidelines and never use spam links or tricks that can lead to penalties.

Is SEO a one-time job or ongoing?

Our On-Page, Off-Page and Technical SEO packages are fixed-price projects. Search keeps changing, so many clients continue with ongoing optimisation afterwards.

How do you report progress?

You get clear reports showing rankings, traffic and the work we've completed, with the next steps we recommend.
