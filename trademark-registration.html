Introduction

Why register a trademark?

A registered trademark gives you the legal right to stop others using your brand name or logo for the same goods or services. It protects your product listings, prevents brand hijacking on Amazon and builds long-term value in your business.

Trademarks are territorial — a UK registration gives no protection in the United States, and vice versa. And under USPTO rule 37 CFR § 2.11, applicants based outside the US must be represented by a licensed US attorney. We manage searches, classes and filings in both countries through accredited attorneys.

FAQs

Trademark Registration FAQs

Why should I register a trademark?

A registered trademark gives you legal ownership of your brand name or logo, helps you stop copycats, and is needed to join Amazon Brand Registry.

Does a UK trademark protect my brand in the US?

No. Trademarks are territorial, so a UK trademark only protects you in the UK and a US trademark only in the US. If you sell in both countries, you need both.

Do I need a US attorney to file a US trademark?

Yes, if you live outside the US. USPTO rules require foreign-domiciled applicants to be represented by a US-licensed attorney. Our US service is filed through accredited US attorneys.

How long does US trademark registration take?

It usually takes around a year or more, depending on USPTO examination times and whether the examiner raises any objections.

How long does UK trademark registration take?

If there are no objections or oppositions, a UK trademark is usually registered in about four months from filing.

Can I join Amazon Brand Registry before my trademark is registered?

In many marketplaces, including the US and UK, Amazon accepts a pending trademark application, so you can usually apply once your application is filed.

What are trademark classes?

Goods and services are grouped into 45 classes, and official fees are charged per class. We help you choose the right classes and write precise descriptions so your brand is properly covered.

Can my trademark application be refused?

Yes — usually if it is too similar to an existing mark or simply describes the product. We run a conflict search before filing to reduce this risk.

How long does a trademark last?

Both US and UK trademarks last 10 years and can be renewed. In the US you also need to file a declaration of use between the fifth and sixth year.
