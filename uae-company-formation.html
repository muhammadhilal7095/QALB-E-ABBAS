Mainland vs free zone

The right choice depends on your activity, customers and plans. Here's a quick guide.

  • Mainland: trade anywhere in the UAESell directly to customers across the UAE and bid for government contracts.
  • Mainland: physical officeUsually requires a registered office space in the emirate.
  • Free zone: simple setupStreamlined licensing and full foreign ownership for many activities.
  • Free zone: flexi-desk optionsMany free zones offer flexi-desk or shared office packages.
  • Both: residence visasInvestor and employee visas linked to your license and office.

FAQs

UAE Company Formation FAQs

Can foreigners own 100% of a UAE company?

Yes. Free zone companies allow full foreign ownership, and since 2021 most mainland business activities can also be 100% foreign-owned.

Should I choose mainland or free zone?

It depends on your business activity, where your customers are, and how you plan to operate. We compare both options for your specific case during your consultation.

Can a free zone company sell to customers in mainland UAE?

Free zone companies mainly trade within their free zone and internationally. Selling directly to the mainland market usually needs a local distributor or extra permits, which is why choosing the right setup matters.

Do I need to visit the UAE to set up a company?

Many setups can be started remotely. If you want a UAE residence visa, you will need to enter the UAE for the medical test and Emirates ID biometrics.

How long does UAE company formation take?

Free zone companies can often be licensed within days to a couple of weeks once documents are ready. Mainland setups can take longer depending on the approvals your activity needs.

What documents do I need?

Usually a passport copy, a passport photo, proof of address and details of your business activity. Some authorities and banks also ask for a CV or bank reference — we give you a checklist for your case.

Is there corporate tax in the UAE?

Yes. Since June 2023 corporate tax is 9% on taxable profits above AED 375,000. Qualifying free zone income can be taxed at 0% if the conditions are met.

Do I need to register for VAT in the UAE?

VAT is 5%. Registration is mandatory once taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000 in 12 months, and voluntary from AED 187,500. We guide you through registration when needed.

Can I get a UAE residence visa through my company?

Yes, most licences allow investor or partner visas. The number of visas depends on the licence and office space you choose.

Can you help with banking after the company is set up?

Yes. We guide you through preparing your corporate bank account application.
