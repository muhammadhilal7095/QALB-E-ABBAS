The IRS Form 5472 Hazard

Many non-US entrepreneurs are told that a US LLC is "tax-free" and mistakenly stop filing paperwork. The IRS mandates that all foreign-owned disregarded single-member LLCs report intercompany transactions annually. Failure to file results in an automated $25,000 fine.
