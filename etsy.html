Introduction

What is Etsy?

Etsy is the leading global marketplace for handmade items, vintage goods, craft supplies and creative, personalised products. Buyers come to Etsy looking for something unique, which makes it a great place to build a small brand with loyal customers.

Etsy only allows items you make, design, or that are vintage or craft supplies — reselling mass-produced goods can get a shop suspended. Strong photos, Etsy SEO, clear shop policies and reliable delivery are what make shops grow. We set up your shop and help it get found.

Etsy fees explained

What selling on Etsy costs

  • 01

    Listing fee

    A small fee per listing, renewed every four months or when the item sells.

  • 02

    Transaction fee

    A percentage of the item price plus shipping on every sale.

  • 03

    Payment processing fee

    Charged on each order through Etsy Payments, depending on your country.

  • 04

    Ads (optional or offsite)

    Etsy Ads spend you choose, plus an Offsite Ads fee only when a sale comes from those ads.

FAQs

Etsy Frequently Asked Questions

What can I sell on Etsy?

Items you make or design yourself, vintage items over 20 years old, craft supplies, and digital downloads. Reselling mass-produced items is not allowed.

Can I open an Etsy shop from Pakistan or another country?

Etsy supports sellers in many countries, but payouts and verification depend on your country. We help you open and verify a shop with the right payment setup.

How does Etsy SEO work?

Etsy ranks listings on how well titles, tags and attributes match a search, plus listing quality, reviews and shop performance. Our Etsy SEO service rewrites titles and tags for the searches buyers make.

Can I sell print-on-demand products on Etsy?

Yes, as long as the designs are your own and you list your production partner on the listing, as Etsy requires. We help you set this up correctly so the shop stays compliant.

How long does it take to get the first sale on Etsy?

It varies by product and niche. Strong photos, accurate tags, competitive pricing and a small Etsy Ads budget usually bring the first sales sooner for a new shop.

Why is my Etsy payment account on reserve?

Etsy may hold part of your payouts for new shops or after a spike in orders or cases. Good delivery, tracking and customer service help reserves be released.

How do Etsy Ads work?

Etsy Ads promote your listings in Etsy search for a daily budget. We set a budget and choose which listings to promote so spend turns into sales.

What is Etsy Star Seller?

A badge for shops that reply to messages quickly, ship on time with tracking and get great reviews. It builds trust with buyers.

My Etsy shop was suspended — can you help?

Yes. We review the reason and prepare an appeal with evidence that your products and processes follow Etsy's rules.
